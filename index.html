FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project with us.

How long does a typical project take?

Most websites launch in 2–4 weeks. Larger web applications usually take 6–12 weeks. You get a clear timeline before we start and weekly updates along the way.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped individually. After a free consultation we send a fixed, transparent quote — no hourly surprises and no hidden fees.

Do you work with clients outside Mexico?

Absolutely. We work with clients across the US, Canada and Europe. We are fully bilingual (English/Spanish) and used to remote collaboration across time zones.

What happens after launch?

Launch is just the beginning. We offer maintenance plans covering updates, security, backups and continuous improvements — or we hand everything over with full documentation.

Can you fix or improve my existing website?

Yes. We audit, repair and optimize existing sites: bug fixes, speed, SEO and redesigns. You don't have to start from scratch.

What technologies do you use?

We choose the right tool for each job — React, Next.js, Node.js, WordPress or Shopify — always prioritizing performance, security and maintainability.
